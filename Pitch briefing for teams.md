## 🗓️ Agenda for the team presentations

The time for teams presenting is 90 minutes for 8 teams (~10min per team).
| Challenge | Teams |
|:---|:---|
| Vehicle Voice Assistant | 3 |
| MachineWhisperer | 3 |
| FarmTwin | 1 |
| The Sentient Home | 1 |

The pitching order within the same challenge will be determined by a lottery draw.

---

Sequence:
1.  **Challenge Intro** (1 minutes): Organizers will give a short introduction before the first team starts.
2.  **Team Pitches**: Each team presents for 3 minutes, followed by a 5-minute Q&A with the jury.
3.  **Popular Vote**: A public vote will take place after all teams have presented.
4.  **Jury Deliberation**: The judges convene to discuss and finalize the scores while the participants take a break.
5.  **Award Ceremony**: Announcement of the winners.


## ⚖️ Jury & Scoring

The panel is made up of 3 managers representing:
*   Bosch
*   AWS
*   MongoDB

### Scoring Criteria
The jury will evaluate the pitches based on the following criteria and weights:

| Category | Criteria | Description | Weight | Voter |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Creativity and Impact | Business & real-world impact | How well the deliverable address the chosen business challenge and how measurable the outcome is | 20 | Jury |
| Creativity and Impact | MVP & Future iteration plan | Readiness and roadmap for production | 10 | Jury |
| Creativity and Impact | Presentation / demo quality | How clearly and convincingly the team explained and demonstrated the project | 10 | Jury |
| Technology | Technical implementation | How well the team built the solution, whether it worked, and how well-architected it is | 10 | Jury |
| Technology | Dataset and data modeling | Schema / data modeling and quality of data used (even if synthetic) | 10 | Jury |
| Technology | Tools & Integrations | Effective use of AWS, MongoDB features and tools* | 10 | Jury |
| Collaboration | Quality Documentation | Clarity of docs/code/readme and explanation for usage and future evolution | 10 | Jury |
| Popular Vote | Popular Vote | Popular vote from the audience | 20 | Popular Vote |
